# Image Segmentation with K-Means Clustering

This exercise is designed to introduce the concepts and techniques of image processing,
clustering, and segmentation using Python. The tasks are intended to guide you through the
process of downloading, manipulating, and analyzing an image, ultimately segmenting it into
distinct regions based on color information.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your results depend entirely on the specific image you choose — report which image
> you used, the k you selected from your own Elbow plot, and why. These can't be
> pre-generated by an AI without seeing your actual image and Elbow curve.

---

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Find an image
Use Google image search to find a `.jpg` image of an arbitrary landscape (size < 500 KB).

### Task 2 — Reshape the image
Read and reshape the image into a 2D array of pixels and RGB colors.

### Task 3 — Find the optimal k
Use the Elbow method to find the optimal number of clusters k for image segmentation.
Report the k you chose and why, based on your own Elbow plot.

### Task 4 — Segment the image
Perform image segmentation using k-means clustering and visualize the result.
